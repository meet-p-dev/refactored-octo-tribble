What MoneyTrack is

A tracker for your own money. It shows your balances, spending, budgets, card bills, debts and savings goals. It never moves money: bank access is read-only, and paying a bill or a person happens in your bank, not in the app.

Not financial advice

Budgets, "safe to spend", projections, card bill amounts, due dates and interest estimates are guides based on the data the app has. They are not financial, tax or legal advice. Always check the amount and due date with your bank or card issuer before you pay.

Bank sync

Bank sync depends on your bank and on Enable Banking. It can be delayed, incomplete or unavailable, and your bank's permission expires and has to be renewed. Only connect accounts you are allowed to access.
